Run a Märklin layout from a Raspberry Pi

A Raspberry Pi HAT and free software that connect a Gleisbox and S88 feedback modules to TrainControl and Rocrail. On your network, the Pi answers like a Central Station.

How it connects

The Pi sits on the board and takes the place of a Central Station. Your driving software talks to it over the network, and it passes everything on to the Gleisbox over CAN. Track contacts come back through S88 modules.

Drive with the software you like

TrainControl

Connects to the Pi as a CS2, or as a CS3 with one setting. It drives locomotives, shows S88 contacts, and imports locomotives and the track diagram from a Rocrail plan. Tested with version 3.0.6.

Rocrail

Runs on the Pi itself, sharing the CAN bus with the gateway, and sees S88 contacts at the same time as TrainControl. It's optional: the board works with TrainControl alone.

What you need

  • COMPUTERRaspberry Pi 3B+ with Raspberry Pi OS Trixie, 64-bit Desktop
  • BOARDThe PiTrainController HAT. Boards aren't on sale yet: v4.3 is still being tested.
  • TRACK POWERA Märklin Gleisbox with its power supply
  • FEEDBACK (OPTIONAL)A commercial S88 module, by ribbon cable or s88-N. A home-built daughterboard is in development but isn't required.

The board powers the Pi through the GPIO header. Don't plug a USB power supply into the Pi while it's on the board.
